Our Approach

A framework for teaching AI literacy honestly

We're not interested in fear-based takes on AI, or uncritical hype either. Our approach is grounded in research, built for students, and honest about what AI can and can't do fairly.

What we offer

Three pillars of our work

Workshops & Curriculum

Hands-on sessions and downloadable lesson plans that walk students through how AI models are trained, where their data comes from, and how bias can quietly take root along the way.

Discussion & Research Circles

Small-group, student-led conversations grounded in published research on AI bias — giving young people real vocabulary and frameworks, not just opinions.

School & Community Partnerships

Working directly with teachers, clubs, and student organizations to bring this content into classrooms and communities that don't have it yet.

Inside a session

How we structure a workshop

Every session follows the same core arc, whether it's a single afternoon or a multi-week program.

01

Start with a real system

We look at an actual AI tool students already use or recognize — not an abstract example.

02

Unpack the data behind it

Where did the training data come from, and whose experiences does — or doesn't — it represent?

03

Discuss who's affected

Students connect the technical to the human — who benefits, who's overlooked, and why it matters.

04

Reflect & take action

Students leave with questions to ask of any AI tool — and often, ideas for their own projects.

Research on AI systems has repeatedly shown that models can reflect and even amplify the biases present in the data used to train them — from facial recognition to hiring tools to content recommendations. Most students interact with these systems daily, but few are ever taught to recognize this. That's the gap we're built to close.
