Team

Right now, it's one student and a growing group of people who believe in this.

Youth AI Literacy Pathways is founder-led and still small. We think that's worth being upfront about.

SG
Founder

Sarah Higashio Gocsei

Rising junior, American School in Japan

Sarah is a multilingual high school student with a strong interest in AI literacy, behavioral economics, and information bias. Growing up in a global, multicultural environment, she became interested in these fields after observing how differences in the information people encounter can shape their perspectives and decisions.

She has published a research paper on AI-driven information inequality, worked as a research intern, presented her findings at the Japan Behaviormetric Society, and been selected as a TEDx Youth speaker. Those experiences made clear that many young people don't have equal access to the knowledge and critical-thinking skills needed to use AI responsibly — which is why she started Youth AI Literacy Pathways (YALPath): to help students from diverse backgrounds understand AI, think critically about misinformation and bias, and engage with emerging technologies responsibly.

"I didn't want this to just be a research paper that sat in a folder. I wanted other students to have the language and the confidence to ask harder questions about the AI tools they already use."

— Sarah Higashio Gocsei, Founder
