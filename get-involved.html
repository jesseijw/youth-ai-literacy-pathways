Get Involved

We're small, student-led, and just getting started — and we'd love your help.

Youth AI Literacy Pathways doesn't run on a big budget or a big team. It runs on people willing to give an afternoon, a classroom, or a share to help this reach more students.

Volunteer

Help run workshops, review curriculum, or lend your time to research and outreach. No prior teaching experience required — just curiosity and reliability.

Share your interest

Partner with your school

Teacher, club leader, or administrator? We'll work with you to bring a workshop or discussion circle to your students.

Start a conversation

Spread the word

Know a student, teacher, or school that would want this? A single introduction can open a door we couldn't open ourselves.

Ongoing, self-paced Fully remote

Outreach Volunteer

Flexible Remote or in-person
